# QueueUp

**QueueUp** is a SaaS platform for managing virtual queues and customer waiting experiences.

Businesses can create and manage queues, while customers join them by scanning a QR code instead of waiting in a physical line.


## 🎯 The Idea

Traditional waiting lines require customers to stay physically present until they are served.

QueueUp replaces this with a simple digital flow:

1. A business creates a queue.
2. QueueUp generates a QR code for the queue.
3. The business displays the QR code at its location.
4. A customer scans the code and joins the queue.
5. The customer receives a queue position.
6. Staff manage waiting customers from a dashboard.
7. The customer is called when it is their turn.


> 🚧 **Status:** Under active development


No application installation is required for customers.
